Model architecture

Inside Franok

A token goes through twelve layers. In each layer, it reads the words before it and uses one of eight feed-forward banks to update its representation.

Franok, from scratchWatch on YouTube ↗

Trace one token

Move the controls. The previous group’s final token shifts which bank processes each of the next eight slots.

Why eight banks?

Franok has 314 million learned parameters. About 50 million participate in one token’s prediction. The rotation sends one slot to each bank in every full group. This gives the model more stored weights than it has to read for a single token.

Where does the next word come from?

The tokenizer splits text into pieces. Attention weighs earlier pieces by their relevance. A gated feed-forward bank transforms the result. After twelve layers, the model assigns a probability to each of 8,192 possible next pieces.

How well does Franok hold a conversation?

In twelve English and Turkish multi-turn checks, the browser model passed four and the desktop model passed three. Both can lose the topic on a follow-up. The optional conversation helper can clarify a greeting or ask the model to retry a confusing answer. Read the test conversations ↗
